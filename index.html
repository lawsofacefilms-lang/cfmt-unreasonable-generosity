The night

You give more than a check when you give this way.

The room on November 16 is full of donors and people who could become donors. The conversation on stage is about generosity that goes past the transaction: getting to know people, thinking outside the box, and making them feel seen.

That idea is the reason the recording matters. The people in the room will hear it once. The film lets CFMT put that same conversation on the website and in front of every donor who was not there.

Approach

Documentation that is also worth watching.

The program
The full conversation, top to bottom, edited as a multi-camera program so it plays like a talk you would choose to watch, not a single locked-off wide shot.
The sound
Clean audio on both speakers is the priority. Audio is secured with ELM before doors open so nothing on stage is lost to a full room.
The room
The crew stays quiet and out of the way. Your guests are there to be hosted, and coverage is planned around that.
The crew
A full team on headset comms, with the same crew lead CFMT has worked with on Gamechangers, the August event, and the donor profiles.
